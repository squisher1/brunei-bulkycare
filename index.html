Welcome to BulkyCare Brunei!

Hello! We are students from Politeknik Brunei, and we are introducing BulkyCare Brunei, a platform idea created to support a cleaner, greener and more sustainable Brunei Darussalam.

Have you ever wondered where to properly dispose of bulky waste items such as old washing machines, sofas, mattresses, refrigerators or bicycles? Disposing of these items can be challenging, especially when you are unsure where to take them or how to get rid of them responsibly.

BulkyCare Brunei is here to help!

Our platform aims to make bulky waste disposal easier by providing useful information and resources to help the public manage their unwanted items responsibly.

What Can You Do With BulkyCare?

Proper Disposal Guidance

Learn how to dispose of different bulky waste items safely and responsibly.

Find Disposal Locations

Discover suitable disposal centres and collection services in Brunei.

Report Illegal Dumping

Help keep our surroundings clean by reporting incidents of illegal dumping.

Waste Management Services

Find companies and organisations that provide waste collection, recycling and disposal services.

Latest News and Updates

Stay informed about waste management, recycling and environmental initiatives.

Community Marketplace

Buy, sell or give away second-hand bulky items, giving unwanted belongings a chance to be reused.

Our Mission

Our goal is to encourage responsible waste management, reduce illegal dumping and promote recycling and reuse within our communities.

Our Project Is Still in Development

Please note that BulkyCare Brunei is currently a student-developed idea and has not yet been fully developed or launched. We are students from Politeknik Brunei who hope to turn this idea into a useful platform that can contribute towards a cleaner and greener Brunei Darussalam.

Although it is still a work in progress, we believe that this initiative has the potential to make bulky waste disposal easier and encourage more responsible waste management within our communities.

We would greatly appreciate the public's support, encouragement and feedback as we work towards developing this idea. Your support could help us take one step closer to making BulkyCare Brunei a reality!

Let's Make a Difference Together!

A cleaner Brunei starts with responsible choices. Together, let's make a difference, one item at a time!
